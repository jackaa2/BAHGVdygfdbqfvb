# Privacy Policy – gta6-clip-robot

gta6-clip-robot is a private tool used only by its owners to organize short video clips.

- It signs in only to the owners' own Google account, to save clip files in their Google Drive and update their own Google Sheet.
- It does not collect, sell or share personal data about anyone.
- It stores no data outside the owners' Google Drive, Google Sheet and this repository.
- Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
